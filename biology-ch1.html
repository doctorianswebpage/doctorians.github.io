Biology Chapter 1: Cell Structure & Function

MDCAT / Pre-Medical Practice MCQs – Instant Scoring & Explanations

1. The powerhouse of the cell is:

Correct: B. Mitochondria – It produces ATP through cellular respiration.

2. Prokaryotic cells lack:

Correct: C. Membrane-bound nucleus – Prokaryotes have nucleoid region, not true nucleus.

3. Which organelle is responsible for protein synthesis?

Correct: A. Ribosome – Site of translation in protein synthesis.
